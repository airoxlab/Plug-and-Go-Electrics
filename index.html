EV charger installation

Home charging, installed right the first time.

Mountain View has more EVs per driveway than almost anywhere. A Level 2 charger needs a dedicated 40 to 60 amp circuit, the right breaker, and a panel that can take the load. We check all three before quoting, so there are no surprises on install day.

  • Load calculation first — many homes do not need a full panel upgrade
  • Hardwired or NEMA 14-50 outlet, whichever suits your charger and garage
  • Permit and inspection handled with the City of Mountain View or your city
  • Charger mounted, wired, tested with your car and app before we leave

All services

Everything a C-10 license covers.

Residential service and small commercial. Pulled to code, permitted where required, tested before we leave.

Panel upgrades

100A to 200A service upgrades, sub-panels for ADUs and garages, breaker replacement, and retiring old Zinsco or Federal Pacific panels.

Lighting

Recessed LED, under-cabinet, dimmers, ceiling fans, exterior and landscape lighting.

Outlets & circuits

New circuits, GFCI and AFCI protection, 240V for ranges, dryers, heat pumps and hot tubs, USB and smart outlets.

Rewiring & remodels

Kitchen and bath remodel wiring, ADU and addition electrical, aluminum and knob-and-tube replacement.

Troubleshooting

Tripping breakers, flickering lights, dead outlets, warm switches. Found, explained, fixed.

Small commercial

Tenant improvements, lighting retrofits, dedicated equipment circuits and workplace EV charging for small offices and shops.
